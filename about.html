The Designer

Joyce Dele Joseph

Founder of JMAGS Fashion Empire

Building a Brand That Stands Out

Joyce Dele Joseph is a Nigerian fashion designer and the founder of JMAGS Fashion Empire, an emerging fashion brand built around creativity, individuality, and self-expression.

She began her fashion journey in 2022, training and developing her skills through hands-on learning across different fashion schools and centres in Lagos. Her experience spans Nigerian native wear, streetwear, menswear, ready-to-wear, and unisex fashion. Along the way she has sharpened her abilities in pattern cutting, garment construction, alterations, and finishing.

As a designer, Joyce is passionate about creating pieces that feel different. She believes fashion should allow people to express themselves, feel confident, and still be comfortable in what they wear. Her creativity comes from experimenting with ideas and combining styles that may not traditionally be put together.

One of her biggest creative goals is to fuse modern streetwear with African-inspired fashion — creating unique pieces that reflect both contemporary style and African identity. She also explores upcycling, transforming existing garments into completely new designs.

Joyce is constantly learning and improving her craft. She believes becoming a good designer is a continuous process — making corrections, researching new techniques, and trying something new. Building her own customer base and seeing people recognise and appreciate her work remain some of the achievements she is most proud of.

Through JMAGS Fashion Empire, she hopes to build a globally recognised fashion brand offering both ready-to-wear and custom-made pieces.

Her Philosophy

“Stay Different.”
